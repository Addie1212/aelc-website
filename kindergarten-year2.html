Why Choose AELC
Kindergarten Year 2

Kindergarten Year 2 is a crucial transition period where children develop independence, social skills, and academic readiness. Our experienced teachers create a supportive environment that balances structured learning with creative exploration, helping each child build confidence and enthusiasm for learning.

  • Pre-Academic Skills
  • Social Development
  • Creative Expression
  • Independence Building

At AELC, we understand that every child develops at their own pace. Our curriculum is designed to challenge and support each child appropriately, ensuring they develop the skills and confidence needed for a successful transition to formal schooling.

Development Areas

Comprehensive Skill Development

Building essential skills across all developmental domains for school readiness.

Pre-Writing Skills

Holding pencils, tracing shapes, and developing fine motor control for writing readiness.

Number Recognition

Counting, number identification, and basic mathematical concepts through hands-on activities.

Letter Awareness

Recognizing letters, sounds, and beginning phonics through songs and interactive games.

Social Skills

Sharing, taking turns, cooperation, and conflict resolution through group activities.

Emotional Intelligence

Identifying feelings, empathy, and self-regulation through stories and role-playing.

Physical Development

Gross motor skills, coordination, and body awareness through active play and games.

Problem Solving

Critical thinking, sequencing, and logical reasoning through puzzles and challenges.

Creativity

Imaginative play, art expression, and innovative thinking through open-ended activities.

Structured Learning

Engaging Daily Activities

A balanced curriculum that includes both structured learning and creative exploration.

Circle Time

Group activities, songs, stories, and discussions that build attention span and social skills.

Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, cutting, and creating with various materials to develop fine motor skills.

Learning Centers

Rotating stations for math, literacy, science, and dramatic play experiences.

Music & Movement

Rhythm activities, dance, and musical games that enhance coordination and creativity.

Outdoor Play

Physical activities, nature exploration, and group games in our safe outdoor space.

Story Time

Reading aloud, retelling stories, and developing comprehension and vocabulary.

Science Discovery

Simple experiments, nature observations, and hands-on exploration of the world.

Group Projects

Collaborative activities that teach teamwork, communication, and leadership skills.
