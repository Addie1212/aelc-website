Kindergarten Programs
Nurturing Growth from Ages 1.5-4

Choose the perfect program for your child's age and development stage. From gentle foundations to skill building and school readiness, AELC guides every child through their most crucial learning years.

Gentle Beginnings

Kindergarten Year 1
Ages 1.5-3

Your toddler's first learning adventure! In our safe, nurturing environment, we focus on building trust, developing motor skills, and creating joyful first experiences with learning.

  • Safe & Secure Environment
  • Individualized Care
  • Sensory Play & Exploration
  • Emotional Development

At AELC, we believe that the foundation of lifelong learning is built in these crucial early years. Our curriculum is designed to stimulate curiosity, encourage communication, and develop essential life skills through play-based learning experiences that are both educational and enjoyable.

Growing Independence

Kindergarten Year 2
Ages 3-4

Kindergarten Year 2 is a crucial transition period where children develop independence, social skills, and academic readiness. Our experienced teachers create a supportive environment that balances structured learning with creative exploration, helping each child build confidence and enthusiasm for learning.

  • Pre-Academic Skills
  • Social Development
  • Creative Expression
  • Independence Building

At AELC, we understand that every child develops at their own pace. Our curriculum is designed to challenge and support each child appropriately, ensuring they develop the skills and confidence needed for a successful transition to formal schooling.

Year 1 Growth Areas

Holistic Development (Ages 1.5-3)

Supporting every aspect of your toddler's growth through age-appropriate activities and experiences.

Motor Skills

Crawling, walking, grasping, and coordination through safe play activities and movement games.

Language Development

First words, sounds, and communication through songs, stories, and interactive conversations.

Cognitive Skills

Problem-solving, memory, and understanding cause-and-effect through sensory play and exploration.

Social & Emotional

Building trust, self-confidence, and basic social skills through loving interactions and routines.

Creative Expression

Art, music, and imaginative play to foster creativity and self-expression from an early age.

Confidence Building

Encouraging independence, celebrating achievements, and fostering a positive self-image through supportive interactions.

Year 2 Learning Areas

Comprehensive Curriculum (Ages 5-6)

Age-appropriate learning that develops the whole child - academically, socially, emotionally, and physically.

Language & Literacy

Phonics, letter recognition, storytelling, and building vocabulary through fun activities.

Mathematics

Numbers, shapes, patterns, and basic counting through hands-on exploration.

Science & Discovery

Exploring nature, weather, animals, and the world around us through observation and experiments.

Arts & Creativity

Drawing, painting, music, dance, and imaginative play to foster creativity and self-expression.

Physical Development

Gross and fine motor skills through games, outdoor play, and coordinated activities.

Social & Emotional

Sharing, cooperation, empathy, and emotional regulation through group activities and play.

Fun-Filled Learning Experiences
