Our Music Program

Why Music Education
Matters

Music education develops creativity, improves cognitive skills, and builds confidence. Our Trinity College London certified program ensures professional training with international standards. Music helps children develop discipline, patience, and the ability to work towards long-term goals.

  • 100% Pass Rate in Trinity Exams
  • Certified Professional Teachers
  • Structured Curriculum & Theory
  • Performance Opportunities

At AELC, we believe that music is for everyone. Our program is designed to be inclusive, ensuring that children of all skill levels can participate and enjoy the learning process while achieving their musical potential.

Instruments We Teach

Choose Your Musical Journey

From traditional to contemporary instruments, we offer comprehensive training in multiple musical disciplines.

Piano

Classical and contemporary piano training with focus on technique, theory, and musicality.

Guitar

Acoustic and electric guitar lessons covering chords, scales, and popular music styles.

Ukulele

Fun and easy-to-learn ukulele lessons perfect for beginners and young students.

Harmonium

Traditional Indian harmonium with focus on classical and devotional music.
