Message from Director

Taru Bharadwaaj

Director, AELC

At AELC – Beyond the School, we believe education is not confined to textbooks or classrooms. It is about nurturing curiosity, creativity, and confidence in every child.

As Director, my vision is to create a safe, joyful, and inspiring environment where children can explore, learn, and grow holistically. From our play school and tuition centre to our activity classes in guitar, art, and English, every program is designed to balance academic excellence with real-life learning experiences.

Located in the heart of Vikaspuri, AELC is more than just a centre it is a community where teachers, parents, and students come together to celebrate growth. With AC classrooms, CCTV security, and a low student-teacher ratio, we ensure personalized attention.

Our mission is simple: to prepare children not only for exams, but for life. By blending academics with creativity, values, and practical skills, we empower them to become confident learners and compassionate individuals.

– Taru Bharadwaaj Director, AELC Beyond the School
