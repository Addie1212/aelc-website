Message from Director

Taru Bharadwaaj

Director, AELC

At AELC – Beyond the School, we believe education is not confined to textbooks. It is about nurturing curiosity, creativity, and confidence in every child.

My vision is to create a safe, joyful, and inspiring environment where children can explore, learn, and grow holistically. From our play school to our activity classes, every program balances academic excellence with real-life learning experiences.

Our mission is simple: to prepare children not only for exams, but for life. By blending academics with creativity, values, and practical skills, we empower them to become confident learners and compassionate individuals.

– Taru Bharadwaaj Director, AELC Beyond the School
