Stages of Learning

The Playschool Curriculum

Carefully designed milestones for ages 1.5 to 6 years.

1.5 - 2.5 Years

Play Group

Focuses on separation anxiety, basic motor skills, sensory play, and social interaction in a warm, loving space.

2.5 - 3.5 Years

Pre-Nursery

Introduction to phonics sounds, colors, shapes, vocabulary building, and structured playway activities.

3.5 - 4.5 Years

Nursery (LKG)

Developing fine motor skills, writing readiness, early mathematics, and creative expression through art.

4.5 - 6.0 Years

Prep (UKG)

Advanced phonics reading, numeric concepts, and school-readiness to ensure a smooth transition to formal schooling.
