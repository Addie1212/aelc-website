Summer Fun

Why Choose AELC
Summer Camp

Our summer camp is designed to keep children engaged, learning, and having fun during the school holidays. With a perfect blend of educational activities, creative projects, outdoor adventures, and social experiences, your child will return to school refreshed, confident, and excited to learn.

  • Creative Arts & Crafts
  • Outdoor Adventures
  • Skill-Building Games
  • Social Development

Led by our experienced teachers and activity specialists, the camp provides a safe, structured environment where children can explore new interests, make new friends, and develop important life skills while having the time of their lives.

Camp Activities

Exciting Daily Adventures

A variety of themed activities that keep every day fresh and exciting.

Arts & Crafts

Painting, clay modeling, DIY projects, and creative expression through various art mediums.

Water Play

Sprinkler games, water balloon fights, and sensory water activities for cooling fun.

Nature Exploration

Nature walks, bug hunting, gardening, and outdoor science discoveries.

Music & Dance

Singing, instrument making, dance parties, and rhythm activities.

Science Experiments

Simple experiments, volcano making, and hands-on science exploration.

Drama & Theater

Role-playing, puppet shows, and creative storytelling performances.

Sports & Games

Team sports, obstacle courses, and cooperative games for physical development.

Story Time

Reading adventures, book making, and creative writing activities.
